## Notes

- Damage shown is the **base** weapon damage. Final damage = `WeaponBase × multStatModifier × HeavyAttackMul × …` (see `BUILD.md`).
- "Avg" is `(min+max)/2`. Some Tier 5/6 weapons trade lower base damage for special on-hit effects (Perceval's Left Blade flipflops poison/bleed; Wintersong Blade has cold).
- Requirements not met → damage clamped down per `ItemRequirementsUtils` (≥-10% per missing point, floor 5%).
- Reinforcement (`+N`) adds `N × minDamageGain` to the floor (typically +1 dmg/level).
