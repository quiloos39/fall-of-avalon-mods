# DPS comparison — actionable scenarios

Generated by `dev/BuildAdvisor/DpsProbe.cs`.

Each scenario applies temporary `StatTweak`s, reads the new modifiers via `Damage.GetStatModifiers`, then discards the tweaks. Numbers below assume **dual-Perceval** (×2 multiplier on per-swing damage) and that crits trigger at the listed rate with the listed crit damage multiplier.

## ⚠️ Caveat on talent modeling

- **Exploitation of Afflictions** in reality is a *target-side `IncomingDamage` multiplier* — it multiplies the final damage by 1.20 (true +20% damage).
- This sim models it as `+0.20` to `MeleeDamageMultiplier` (additive in the AddPreMultiply bucket). That **underestimates** the gain. The actual +20% would multiply *after* the multStatMod calc, giving the full +20%.
- **First Blood** is "Physical Damage +X%" — likely also a multiplier in the chain (also under-estimated here).
- Bottom line: the **modeled** gains here are *conservative*; the **real** gains are larger.
